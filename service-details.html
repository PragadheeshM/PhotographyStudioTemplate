Overview

Built for Epic Productions

Commercial Studio B was purpose-designed for large-scale advertising, fashion, and commercial film productions. The 24ft open-truss ceiling accommodates full overhead rigging, while the 40ft drive-in bay lets you bring in vehicles, large props, or production trucks without a second thought.

The integrated 30ft chroma-key green screen, permanently rigged Arri LED lighting, and 200A power service make it the most technically capable studio in our portfolio - yet it's available to book by the hour, half-day, or full day.
