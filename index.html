Our Story

A Legacy of Creative Excellence

Founded in 2010 by award-winning photographer Marcus Ellison, High-End Photography Studio Rental was born from a single vision: to give every photographer access to the same calibre of space that elite commercial studios enjoy.

Today, our flagship location spans 18,000 sq ft across Manhattan's Meatpacking District, housing 14 purpose-built studios, a curated prop library of 2,000+ items, and an in-house retouching lab.
