Our Story

About HEPS

Born from a photographer's frustration, built into New York's most-awarded studio complex.

Our Mission

Democratising World-Class Studio Access

In 2010, Marcus Ellison was an emerging commercial photographer tired of paying $8,000/day for Manhattan's top studios - spaces that remained empty 70% of the time. He had a radical idea: what if those spaces could be shared, maintained to the same elite standard, and made accessible to every tier of photographer?

That idea became High-End Photography Studio Rental. Today, HEPS spans 18,000 sq ft in the Meatpacking District with 14 studios, a prop library of 2,000+ items, and a team of 42 creatives who've collectively shot for Vogue, Nike, Apple, and the Met Gala.
